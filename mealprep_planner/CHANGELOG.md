# Changelog

- MealPrep Planner now shows up in the sidebar for every household member, not only for admins.

- Recipes: "Wann passt es?" (categories) and "Tags" are now two separate sections; the Mittag/Abend switches are gone. A recipe without a category fits every meal.
- Week plan is much shorter: one line per meal, everything else behind the "⋯" button.
- Settings are grouped into sections you can open and close; categories and tags each have their own simple list.
- "1 Portion" instead of "1 Portionen".
