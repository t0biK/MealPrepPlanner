# Changelog

## 0.17.0

- TikTok: when the caption only lists ingredients, the app now also reads the spoken text of the video, so the cooking steps show up in the draft.
- YouTube: the full video description is read too, so recipes written there are picked up without pasting anything.
- With AI switched off, video links work as before.

## 0.16.1

- MealPrep Planner now shows up in the sidebar for every household member, not only for admins.

## 0.16.0

- Recipes: "Wann passt es?" (categories) and "Tags" are now two separate sections; the Mittag/Abend switches are gone. A recipe without a category fits every meal.
- Week plan is much shorter: one line per meal, everything else behind the "⋯" button.
- Settings are grouped into sections you can open and close; categories and tags each have their own simple list.
- "1 Portion" instead of "1 Portionen".

## 0.1.0 – 0.15.0

- First versions: recipes and import, ratings, week planner, Bring! shopping list, today page, household targets, canteen and leftovers.
